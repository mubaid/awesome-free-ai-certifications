## What "free with certificate" means

Transparency first, because "free" varies by provider:
- **Listed** means the provider page advertised free access and a completion certificate, badge, or Trailhead-style achievement when the entry was added.
- **Check before you start.** Most courses need a free account. A few may gate exams, ID verification, or certificates behind login, region, or policy changes.
- **Found a paywall or dead link?** Please [add a course or fix](CONTRIBUTING.md) via `data/course_catalog.csv` — or open an issue with the course URL. That is how this list stays trustworthy.
